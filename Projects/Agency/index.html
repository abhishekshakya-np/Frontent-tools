Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our stock screening platform.

How often is the stock data updated?

Our platform provides real-time data updates throughout trading hours. Historical data is updated daily after market close.

Is there a free version available?

Yes, we offer a free tier with basic screening features. Premium plans unlock advanced filters and portfolio analytics.

Which stock exchanges are supported?

Currently, we support all stocks listed on the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE). We're working on expanding to other regional markets.

Can I save my custom filters?

Absolutely! Premium users can save unlimited custom filters and set up alerts for when stocks match their criteria.
