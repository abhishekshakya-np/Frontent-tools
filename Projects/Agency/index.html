Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Questions

Get quick answers to the most frequently asked questions about our platform and features.

How often is the stock data updated?

Real-time data updates occur throughout trading hours, while historical data is updated daily after market close.

Is there a free version available?

Yes, we offer a free tier with basic screening features, while premium plans unlock advanced filters and portfolio analytics.

Which stock exchanges are supported?

We currently support all stocks listed on the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) and are expanding to other regional markets.

Can I save my custom filters?

Premium users can save unlimited custom filters and set up alerts for when stocks match their criteria.
