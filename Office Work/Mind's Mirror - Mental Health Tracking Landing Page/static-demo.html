Mind's Mirror

A mental health tracking and goal management system designed for individuals managing bipolar disorder and other mental health conditions.

Core Features

Mood Tracking

Track depression, anxiety, irritability, elevated mood, sleep hours, and therapy sessions with severity levels from 1-10.

Goal Management

Create goals with milestones, deliverables, and acceptance criteria. Track progress and manage workload based on mental health status.

Crisis Detection

Automatic wellness analysis with crisis risk detection. Workload reduction when mental health thresholds are exceeded.

Calendar View

Visualize mood patterns with interactive calendar. View daily, weekly, and monthly mood trends.

Focus Sessions

Timed focus sessions with break reminders. Adjust session length based on current mental health status.

Medication Tracking

Track medication adherence and side effects. Set reminders and monitor effectiveness over time.

Goal Categories

Health & Wellness

Physical health, exercise, nutrition, and wellness goals

Location

Travel, moving, and location-based objectives

Career

Professional development and career advancement

Education

Learning, courses, certifications, and skill development
